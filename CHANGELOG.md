# Changelog

- Added an authoritative battlefield timeline with player, companion, and hostile intent, target, readiness, recovery, interruption, and tactical effects.
- Kept primary combat actions visible through a sticky Combat Now deck and responsive action tray.
- Added authoritative faction, allegiance, rank, territory, party, and Sprawl 15 civic-state presentation.
